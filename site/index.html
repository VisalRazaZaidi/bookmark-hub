Recognise the page before you open it.

With previews on, each card shows the page's own preview image and real title. Pages without one get a tile built from the domain, so no card is ever a blank rectangle.

  • Optional and local. Previews are fetched straight from the site and cached in your browser.
  • Names for untitled bookmarks. Missing titles are filled from the page itself.
  • Turn it off any time. Clearing the cache or revoking the permission takes one click.

Your bookmarks are not our business model.

Bookmark Hub has no account, no analytics and no server. It reads your bookmarks through the browser's own API, on your device, and stops there.

Nothing leaves the browser

The extension has no backend and sends no bookmark data anywhere. It works offline; only optional previews make a network request.

Previews only when you ask

Page previews are off until you click Enable previews. Each bookmarked page is fetched directly from its own site and cached locally.

Site icons can be turned off

Icons come from Google's favicon service, which means each bookmarked domain is sent to Google. One switch in Settings stops that.

Questions worth asking

Does it replace my new tab page?

No. Bookmark Hub opens its own tab when you click the toolbar icon or press Alt+B. Your new tab page stays exactly as it is.

Will it change or move my bookmarks?

It reads them and displays them. The only write is when you click Delete on a card, and that action is reversible with Undo for eight seconds. Nothing is reorganised, renamed or moved automatically.

Why does it ask for access to all sites?

Only for the optional page previews. The browser shows that prompt after you click Enable previews, because the extension needs to read each bookmarked page's title, description and preview image. It never runs on the pages you visit and you can revoke it in Settings.

Where does the site-icon data come from?

By default, favicons are loaded from Google's favicon service, so each bookmarked domain is sent to Google. The Settings panel has a Site icons switch that turns this off; the coloured tiles still work without it.

Can I share bookmarks with someone who does not use Bookmark Hub?

Yes. Share the current folder, search result or a hand-picked selection as a link list, Markdown or a standard bookmark file. The file imports into Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari with folder names intact. Sharing is generated locally; nothing is uploaded.

Does it work with a large bookmark collection?

Yes. Cards render in chunks as you scroll, and search runs on the bookmarks already loaded in the page. Thousands of bookmarks stay responsive.

How do I remove it and its data?

Use Settings > Page previews > Clear cache, then remove the extension from your browser's extensions page. Settings and the preview cache are stored locally by the extension and disappear with it.
