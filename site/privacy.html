Privacy policy

Bookmark Hub is a browser extension that shows and searches the bookmarks already saved in your browser. This page explains exactly what it reads, what it stores and what leaves your device.

The short version: your bookmarks stay in your browser. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no Bookmark Hub server. The developer receives no data.

Sharing is a local export. It never uploads anything and never contacts a Bookmark Hub server.

The only network requests are the ones you opt into: optional page previews, which go directly to the bookmarked sites, and site icons, which go to Google's favicon service unless you turn them off.

What the extension reads

With the bookmarks permission, Bookmark Hub reads your browser's bookmark tree: each bookmark's title, URL, folder and date added, plus folder names. It uses that information only to draw its own page: the folder tree, counts, cards, previews and search results.

Bookmark Hub does not read your browsing history, the pages you currently have open, form data, passwords or cookies. It does not run on the websites you visit.

What is stored on your device

Two things are saved in the extension's local storage, in your browser profile:

  • Settings, such as theme, layout, sort order and whether site icons are on.
  • The optional preview cache: page titles, descriptions, preview-image addresses and icon addresses that the extension fetched, so it does not fetch the same page twice.

This storage is local to your browser. It is not synced by Bookmark Hub and is not sent to the developer. Removing the extension deletes it.

Optional page previews

Page previews are off until you click Enable previews. At that point the browser asks you to grant the optional “all sites” permission, which the extension needs to read each bookmarked page's <head>.

When previews are on, the extension requests each bookmarked page directly from the site that hosts it. It reads the page title, description, preview image address and site icon, then caches the result locally. Requests are sent without your cookies (credentials: "omit").

Those websites can see the request the same way they see any other visit: your IP address, browser user agent and the page requested. Bookmark Hub does not send them your bookmark list or any other information about you.

You can stop this at any time with Settings > Page previews > Turn off, and delete what was cached with Clear cache.

Site icons and Google's favicon service

To show a recognisable icon on each card, Bookmark Hub requests it from Google's favicon service (google.com/s2/favicons) by default. That means the domain of each bookmarked site is sent to Google, together with the normal technical details of the request.

Google's handling of that request is covered by Google's own privacy policy. If you would rather not send domains to Google, turn off Site icons in Settings. Cards still work; they use a coloured tile built from the site name instead.

Sharing

Sharing is a local export. When you choose a folder, a search result or a selection, Bookmark Hub builds the link list, Markdown text or bookmark file inside the page. Nothing is uploaded, there is no share server, and the developer never sees what you share.

The recipient receives only what you send: bookmark titles, URLs and folder names. The bookmark file also contains the standard “date added” timestamp. Page-preview images, settings and your browsing activity are never part of a share.

Deleting bookmarks

Bookmark Hub only deletes a bookmark when you click the Delete button on a card. It never deletes in the background. Each click removes exactly one bookmark, identified by its unique browser id, not by its URL.

After a delete, an Undo button stays available for eight seconds. Undo recreates the bookmark in its original folder and position. Deleting a duplicate does not delete the other copies of the same link.

What is never collected

  • No account, sign-up or personal identifiers.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or tracking pixels.
  • No advertising and no selling or sharing of data with third parties.
  • No bookmark data sent to the developer or any Bookmark Hub server, because there is no server.
  • No remote code: all JavaScript is bundled inside the extension package.
The extension's only permissions are bookmarks and storage, plus the optional all-sites permission used for previews after you enable them. It deliberately does not request the tabs permission.

Retention and how to clear your data

Settings and cached previews stay on your device until you remove them. To clear everything:

  • Open Bookmark Hub's Settings and use Page previews > Clear cache to delete cached page data.
  • Use Turn off to revoke the optional all-sites permission.
  • Remove Bookmark Hub from your browser's extensions page to delete its local storage. Any bookmarks you deleted yourself remain deleted; the browser's own bookmark data is otherwise untouched.

Children

Bookmark Hub is a general-purpose productivity tool. It is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the extension's behaviour changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. Material changes will also be noted in the extension's release notes on its store listing.

Contact

Questions about privacy or data handling: use the Support tab on the Bookmark Hub listing in the Chrome Web Store. That is the monitored contact channel; no personal email address is published here.

This policy applies to the Bookmark Hub extension for Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox, and to this website. The website sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Its two typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of that request; the extension itself bundles its fonts locally and makes no such request.
